Fireplace TV

A cozy fire for your Apple TV

Five drawn fires, each with its own sound. This app is for Apple TV only.

Five fires

Wood logs in a brick fireplace, glowing embers, a campfire at night, a wood stove and a line of gas burners.

Just the fire on your screen

No ads and nothing over the picture. The fire fills the whole wide screen in high resolution.

Seamless loops

Each scene is a 60 second loop, so you never see a jump.

Sleep timer

15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes. Then the picture and the sound fade away softly.

Your music keeps playing

Put on your own music and let the fire burn along with it.

Works without internet

Every scene and every sound is inside the app. A clock shows only if you turn it on.
