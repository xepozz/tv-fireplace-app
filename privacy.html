Fireplace TV

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026.

Fireplace TV has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not sell any data.

Usage statistics

The app sends anonymous usage events to our own server, hubbuh.ru, to see how people use the app. An event says that the app was opened, with a random identifier and the device details: the model, the system version, the language, the time zone, the screen and the network type. An event never contains your name, your email or your location. The events are not sold and not shared with advertisers. To stop them, delete the app. The app works without internet: all scenes and sounds are inside the app.

Data that stays on your Apple TV

The app keeps its settings, such as the last scene, only on your Apple TV. Removing the app removes them.

This site

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sends page views to our own server, hubbuh.ru, with a random identifier in the cookie ab_uid. It has no other cookies and no other trackers.

Children

The app does not ask anyone, including children, for personal data.
