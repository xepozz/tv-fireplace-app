Fireplace TV

Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026.

Fireplace TV collects nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not collect, store or sell any data.

No network

The app does not connect to the internet. All scenes and sounds are inside the app, and the app sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.

Data that stays on your Apple TV

The app keeps its settings, such as the last scene, only on your Apple TV. Removing the app removes them.

This site

This site has no cookies, no trackers and no external scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
